Offerings

Six places margin disappears, and what I do about each.

Every engagement starts with the numbers you already have — POS exports, purchase registers, settlement statements — and ends with a process your managers actually follow.

01 / INVENTORY

Inventory Management

Stock that walks out the back door without appearing on any report. I close the gap between what your POS depleted and what your store actually holds.

  • Theoretical vs. actual usage variance
  • Par levels and reorder points per SKU
  • Wastage, spoilage and yield tracking
  • Closing stock audit discipline
  • Recipe costing and portion control
02 / MONEY TRAIL

Payment Reconciliation

The full journey from a customer's payment to money landing in your bank. Most outlets never check the middle of that chain — and that is exactly where it goes missing.

  • POS sales vs. aggregator payouts
  • Swiggy & Zomato commission audits
  • UPI, card and wallet settlement matching
  • MDR, TDR and TCS deduction checks
  • Unsettled batches and chargeback recovery
03 / REPORTING

Sales Reporting & MIS

One daily report your whole team reads the same way. No two managers quoting different numbers for the same day.

  • Daily sales and cash MIS
  • Outlet-wise and day-part revenue mix
  • ATV, covers and same-store growth
  • Automated dashboards from your POS
  • Weekly and monthly business reviews
04 / MARGIN

Food Cost & Margin

Food cost is not one number — it is a dozen decisions compounding. I unpick which ones are costing you points of gross profit.

  • Theoretical vs. actual food cost
  • Yield testing for semi-finished items
  • Menu engineering and item-level GP
  • Vendor rate benchmarking
  • Outlet-level P&L reconstruction
05 / CONTROLS

Audits & SOPs

Written standards, and an audit that actually catches drift. Processes that survive staff turnover instead of leaving with your best manager.

  • Operational audit checklists
  • Cash handling and shift controls
  • SOP documentation per station
  • Opening and closing discipline
  • Staff training and accountability
06 / GROWTH

Expansion Support

Before you sign the next lease: whether the unit economics hold, and whether your current operation can absorb another site.

  • Franchise due diligence before you buy
  • Break-even and payback modelling
  • Site and unit-economics review
  • New outlet opening checklists
  • Area manager structure and span

Where it leaks Commission charged above contract, orders marked delivered but never settled, refunds debited twice, payouts netted against disputes you were never told about. Checked line by line against your own POS data.

Who this is for

Multi-outlet owners

Three or more sites where performance varies and you cannot tell whether it is the location, the manager, or the numbers.

Franchise partners

Running someone else's brand on your own capital, and needing your unit to actually return what the model promised.

Growing brands

Scaling past the point where one founder can hold operations in their head, and needing systems before the next opening.

Single-site operators

Good revenue, thin profit, and no clear view of where the gap between the two is sitting.

Approach

Diagnose, fix, systemise, hand over.

01

Diagnose

I take your last three months of POS exports, purchase registers and settlement statements, and reconcile them against each other. No site visit needed to start.

Output: leakage report

02

Fix

We work the largest gaps first — the two or three items carrying most of the loss. Recovery of past money where it is recoverable, and controls so it stops.

Output: recovered margin

03

Systemise

The fix becomes a routine: a written SOP, a daily report, an audit cadence. Built on tools you already pay for, not new software you must learn.

Output: SOPs + dashboards

04

Hand over

Your managers run it, not me. I train the team, leave the documentation, and stay available for review cycles if you want them.

Output: your team owns it

About

I trained as an engineer. Now I fix businesses instead of engines.

I'm Shubham Jain. I work as a Regional Manager at Nothing Before Coffee, where I'm responsible for sales performance, profitability and operational standards across multiple cafés and the Area Managers who run them.

I also own and operate an NBC franchise café myself. That matters more than it sounds. I've sat on both sides of the table — setting standards from the corporate side, and absorbing the cost of them as the owner. When I recommend a change, I've already paid for it personally.

My degree is in Automobile Engineering from SRM Institute of Science and Technology, where I co-authored published research on multi-element automotive wing design — work later cited in a granted US patent. I moved into operations during COVID and found the work I actually wanted: diagnosing why a system underperforms, then rebuilding it so it doesn't.

What I care about is building systems rather than repeating tasks. If a problem recurs monthly, it isn't a problem — it's a missing process. That's the lens I bring to inventory, reconciliation and reporting, and it's why engagements end with your team running the fix instead of calling me back.
